A LITTLE HELP

Good questions.
Simple answers.

Everything you need to know about exploring the demo.

Can I buy these products?

No. Products are fictional, and checkout creates only a local demo order. Nothing is charged or shipped.

Where is my cart saved?

In browser local storage. Your bag, favorites, demo profile and orders survive refreshes on the same origin. Another browser or port has separate demo data.

Does the demo password authenticate me?

The account pages validate fictional credentials but do not authenticate you or save your password. Real authentication will be implemented in the backend exercise.

How do I reset the demo?

Use “Reset demo data” in the footer. It clears local orders and profile, empties your cart and favorites, and restores the original catalog.

Can I manage products?

The demo management screens let you create, edit and delete products in this browser. They are publicly visible demonstrations, not a protected admin system.

Will I receive an order email?

No emails are sent. You can find your local order in the demo orders page.

PRIVACY IN THIS DEMO

Your browser.
Your demo data.

Use fictional information

This learning project is not intended to collect real personal information. Use a fictional name, example.com email and fictional delivery address.

Local storage

The frontend stores the catalog, cart, favorites, demo profile and demo orders in local storage under “devstore.demo.v1”. Fictional password fields demonstrate validation only and are never saved or sent. No card details are requested. No data is sent to an API by the current demo.

Clear your data

Use “Reset demo data” in the footer or clear this site's browser storage. Storage belongs to this origin and is accessible to anyone using the same browser profile.

Fonts and external resources

Typography uses system fonts. Product illustrations are CSS. This demo does not require an external font or image provider.

Future implementation

Real accounts and backend persistence need an appropriate privacy policy and data-handling design before public use.

Demonstration only
